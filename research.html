Research

My research aims to promote responsible ways of building, deploying and using artificial intelligence algorithms, with an emphasis on understanding the social and ethical implications of these activities.

My Master's thesis in the Department of Sciences at the University of Quebec in Montreal was on co-creating a digital assistant for employees in the financial industry. This project in collaboration with the National Bank of Canada involved implementing a smart search and a chatbot to enhance information retrieval and improve workplace efficiency.

I focus specifically in these areas:

  • Uncovering LLMs Socio-Technical Limitations—investigating LLMs limitations such as bias, misinformation, and their socio-technical impacts in healthcare and education, including mitigation strategies for safer deployment.
  • LLM Finetuning—adapting pre-trained large language models to perform better on specific tasks.
  • Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)—merges data retrieval with generative AI to get more precise answers.
  • AI fairness Analysis—assess ML models fairness using metrics such as F1 score.
  • Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL)—uses neural networks to help agents make optimal decisions in complex environments using trial and error.
